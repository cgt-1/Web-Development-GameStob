About GameStoB

We started small. And grew with our players.

Born from a love of games


GameStoB was founded in 2015 from the belief that buying games should be as enjoyable as playing them. Play more, Pay less. What started as a small digital storefront has grown into a full-scale retailer with over 5,000 titles and physical stores across Indonesia.

We've always been player-first. From how we source our keys to how we design our stores.

Today, GameStoB continues to grow globally, with a mission to make great games accessible to anyone, anywhere.
